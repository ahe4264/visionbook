L03049: To say that $x$ approximates $x_{0}$ to $n$ decimal places does not mean that the first $n$ decimal places of $x$ and $x_{0}$ will be the same when the numbers are rounded to $n$ decimal places. For example, $x=1.084$ approximates $x_{0}=1.087$ to two decimal places because $\left|x-x_{0}\right|=0.003$ (< 0.005 ). However, if we round these values to two decimal places, then we obtain $x \approx 1.08$ and $x_{0} \approx 1.09$. Thus, if you approximate a number to $n$ decimal places, then you should display that approximation to at least $n+1$ decimal places to preserve the accuracy.
