Polar rectangle  R = {(r,θ)} Hover the arcs, rays and shaded region for details; click for a short explanation. Key idea: R is a rectangle only in the (r, θ) plane. In the xy-plane its constant-r sides bow into arcs of lengths r₁Δθ and r₂Δθ — that stretching factor r is exactly why dA = r dr dθ.
