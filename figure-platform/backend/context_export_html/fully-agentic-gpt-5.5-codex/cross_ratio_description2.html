Cross-Ratio Projection

Central projection preserves the ordered cross-ratio.

Definition

CR(P) = |P3-P1||P4-P2| / |P3-P2||P4-P1|

R1 at infinity: CR(R) = |R4-R2| / |R3-R2|

Geometry
