Pinhole Camera Geometry

Camera-centered axes, image plane position, and the names used in perspective projection.

Projection plane

The image or projection plane is placed one focal length from the pinhole. Rays through the camera center meet this plane at the perspective image coordinates.

Projection equations x = f X / Z y = f Y / Z

Right-handed convention: X cross Y points along +Z, with camera Y upward. Pixel rows often use v = c_y - f_y Y / Z because row coordinates increase downward.
