sp3d Hybridization — Phosphorus Pentachloride, PCl5

One 3s, three 3p and one 3d orbital of phosphorus mix to give five equivalent sp3d hybrid orbitals pointing at the vertices of a trigonal bipyramid — exactly the five bonding directions seen in the molecule.
