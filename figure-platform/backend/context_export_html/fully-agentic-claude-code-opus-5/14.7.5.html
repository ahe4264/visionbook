Change of variables: a rectangle S in the uv-plane and its image R in the xy-plane

The transformation T(u,v) = (x(u,v), y(u,v)) carries each side of the rectangle to a curve. Edit the formulas, or drag the blue corner dots of S, and watch how much T stretches area — that stretching factor is the Jacobian.

Available in the formulas: + − * / ^ ( ), sin cos tan asin acos atan atan2 exp log(=ln) sqrt abs sinh cosh tanh pow pi e. Drag a corner dot to resize S (the opposite corner stays put), or drag inside S to move it. Shrink S and the ratio area(R)/area(S) converges to |∂(x,y)/∂(u,v)| at (u0, v0).
