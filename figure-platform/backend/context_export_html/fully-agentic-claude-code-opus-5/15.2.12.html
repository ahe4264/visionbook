The line integral ∫C F · dr is the signed area under this curve. The shaded region belongs to the active panel; the dashed curve is the other panel.

Why the two answers differ. |F| = √(cos2x + sin2x) = 1 everywhere, so ∫C F·dr = ∫C (F·T) ds = ∫C cos θ ds, and the answer can never exceed the arc length L in size. In (a) the field at x = −π/2 is exactly ⟨0, −1⟩ — it points straight down, the same way the curve is traversed — so cos θ = 1 and the integral equals the length, 1. In (b) the arc is long and mostly climbs with the field, so the total is much larger. Because ∂(sin x)/∂x = cos x ≠ 0 = ∂(cos x)/∂y, F is not conservative: the value really does depend on the path, not just on the endpoints. The one piece that is path independent is ∫C cos x dx = sin xend − sin xstart.
