The cross-ratio — a projective invariant

Four rays leave the centre of projection O. Every straight line that cuts the pencil meets it in four points, and those four points always have the same cross-ratio. Line ℓR is parallel to the ray through O, P1, Q1, so R1 is pushed out to infinity — the cross-ratio survives that too.

Drag: the four points P1–P4 (they slide along ℓP and swing the rays) · the centre O · any crimson square handle to slide or turn a transversal.
