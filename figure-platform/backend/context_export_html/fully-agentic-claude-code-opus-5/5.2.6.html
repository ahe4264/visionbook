Volume by slicing: V = ∫ab A(x) dx

The solid S runs from x = a to x = b. Cut it by planes perpendicular to the x-axis into n slabs S1, …, Sn. Each slab is nearly a right prism of cross-sectional area A(xk*) and thickness Δxk, so V ≈ Σ A(xk*)Δxk.
