The Gouy balance — weighing unpaired electrons

A sample tube hangs from one arm of a beam balance into the gap of an electromagnet, where the field is strongly inhomogeneous. A paramagnetic sample is pulled into the field and appears heavier; a diamagnetic sample is pushed out and appears lighter. Calibrated weights are added to (or removed from) the counter-pan until the beam re-levels, and the apparent mass change gives the magnetic susceptibility — and hence the number of unpaired electrons.
