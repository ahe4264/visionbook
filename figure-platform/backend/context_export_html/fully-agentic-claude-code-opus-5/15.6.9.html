Flux through a surface patch: F·n ΔSk as a volume of fluid per unit time

The fluid moves with velocity F. In one unit of time the fluid that crosses the patch σk in the direction of the unit normal n fills an oblique column whose volume is F·n ΔSk. Drag the patch over the surface to watch n reorient and ΔSk change; drag anywhere else to rotate the view.
