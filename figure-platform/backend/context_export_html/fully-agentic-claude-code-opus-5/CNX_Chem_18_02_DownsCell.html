The Downs cell — making sodium metal from molten NaCl

Electrolysis of molten sodium chloride at about 600 °C. Press Play to drive the cell: Cl− ions migrate inward to the central anode and Cl2 gas bubbles up the collector tube, while Na+ ions migrate outward to the cathode and the liquid sodium floats up into the collecting ring. Click any part of the cell for its half-reaction.
