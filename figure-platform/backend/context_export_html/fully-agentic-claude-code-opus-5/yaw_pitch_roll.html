Yaw, pitch and roll: Euler angles of a camera

A right-handed world frame is anchored at the camera centre (0,0,0): Y up, X lateral, Z along the optical axis. Three elementary rotations — yaw about Y, pitch about X, roll about Z — compose into the camera rotation R. The tilted image plane (with local axes x, y) sits at z = f in the camera frame; the world point P projects along the dashed ray to the image point p.
