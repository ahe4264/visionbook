Fluid force on a submerged vertical surface

Slicing the surface into thin horizontal strips turns the varying pressure into a Riemann sum  F ≈ ∑ ρ h(xk*) w(xk*) Δxk, whose limit is the integral. All lengths in feet; ρ = 62.4 lb/ft3 is the weight density of water.

F  ≈  n ∑ k = 1  ρ h(xk*) w(xk*) Δxk   ⟶   F = ∫ab ρ h(x) w(x) dx   (here h(x) = x, depth measured from the fluid surface)
