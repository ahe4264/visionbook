Pinhole camera geometry — world, camera and image coordinates

(a) A scene point P projects through the pinhole at the world origin. The physical projection plane sits a distance f behind the pinhole and records an inverted image; the virtual camera plane sits a distance f in front and carries the same image upright. (b) The virtual camera plane carries two 2-D frames at once: continuous camera coordinates (x, y) centred on the principal point, and discrete pixel indices (n, m) counted from the image corner.
