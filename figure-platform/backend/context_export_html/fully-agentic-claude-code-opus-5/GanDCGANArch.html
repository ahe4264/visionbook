DCGAN — the deep convolutional GAN architecture

Two mirrored all-convolutional pipelines. The generator projects a 100-dimensional latent vector z into a 4×4×1024 volume and then fractionally strides (transposed-convolves) its way up to a 64×64×3 image. The discriminator takes a real image x or a generated image x* and strides back down to a single number, squashed by a sigmoid into Pr(real). Every shape below is computed from the convolution parameters, not hard-coded.
