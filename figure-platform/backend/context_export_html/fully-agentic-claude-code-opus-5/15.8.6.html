Curl F(P₀): the paddle-wheel interpretation

A tiny paddle wheel is held at P₀ in a swirling fluid. Its angular speed is ω = ½ (curl F(P₀) · n), where n is the spindle direction — so the wheel spins fastest when the spindle points along curl F(P₀), and the curl vector's length is twice that maximum spin rate.
