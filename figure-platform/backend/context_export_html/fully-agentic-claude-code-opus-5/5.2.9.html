The Disk Method

Revolving the region R under y = f(x) about the x-axis: every cross-section perpendicular to the axis is a disk of radius f(x).

The disk at position x has radius r = f(x) and area A(x) = π[f(x)]². A slab of thickness dx contributes the volume A(x) dx; adding the slabs from a to b gives the integral. Drag the slider to sweep the disk along the axis, or edit f, a, b to reshape the solid.
