Epipolar geometry of a stereo pair

Two cameras with centres O₁, O₂ view a scene point P. The three points span the epipolar plane; the baseline O₁O₂ pierces each image plane at the epipoles e₁, e₂; and the epipolar plane cuts each image plane in an epipolar line through the epipole and the image point. Everything below is computed live from O₁, O₂, P and the two camera poses — nothing is drawn by hand.

Drag the cyan point P to move it in 3D (hold Shift while dragging to push it in depth, along z). Drag the background to tilt the view. The epipolar plane, the epipoles and both epipolar lines are recomputed every frame.
