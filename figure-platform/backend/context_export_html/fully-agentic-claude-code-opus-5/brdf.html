The BRDF: how a surface turns incoming light into outgoing radiance

A ray of incident light ℓin(λ) strikes a point on a surface whose normal is n and whose local tangent frame is (p, q). Instead of bouncing into one mirror direction, the energy is scattered over the whole hemisphere of outgoing directions. The length of each green arrow is the radiance actually reflected into that direction, computed live from an anisotropic microfacet BRDF.

Drag anywhere on the figure to swing the incoming ray ℓin around the hemisphere  ·  Shift + drag (or right-drag) to orbit the viewpoint  ·  every arrow is recomputed from the BRDF each frame.
