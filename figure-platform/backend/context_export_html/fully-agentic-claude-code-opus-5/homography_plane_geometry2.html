Why a plane in the scene gives a 3×3 homography

The world frame is placed on the planar surface with its Z‑axis along the surface normal, so every scene point is (X, Y, 0). That zero third coordinate deletes the third column of the 3×4 camera matrix, and the projection collapses to one invertible 3×3 matrix H taking (X, Y) straight to the image point (x, y). Every line, quadrilateral and number below is computed from the real pinhole geometry.
