From the uv-plane to curves on a parametric surface

A parametrization r(u, v) = ⟨x(u,v), y(u,v), z(u,v)⟩ maps the flat parameter domain on the left onto the surface on the right. A vertical line u = constant maps to the orange constant-u curve; a horizontal line v = constant maps to the purple constant-v curve. The whole families of these curves are the curvilinear coordinate grid drawn in gray on the surface.
