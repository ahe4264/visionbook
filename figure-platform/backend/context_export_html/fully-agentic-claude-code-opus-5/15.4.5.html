Extending Green's Theorem to a region with a hole

The positive orientation of a multiply connected region: the outer boundary C1 counterclockwise, the inner boundary C2 clockwise — so that the region R always lies to the left. Cutting R into two simply connected pieces R′ and R″ proves it: the cut is traversed once in each direction, so its contributions cancel.
