Motion of a 3D point and the motion of its image

A point P = (X, Y, Z) moves with velocity Ṗ = (Ẋ, Ẏ, Ż). The green ray from the camera centre O through P pierces the image plane (at depth Z = f) at the image point p. The dashed ray shows where the displaced point projects, so the red arrow ṗ at p is the projection of Ṗ — not a rescaled copy of it.
