The lower edge of R is described by two different formulas — one to the left of the y-axis, one to the right. A vertical strip (red) therefore starts on a different curve depending on which half it lies in, so R must be split into two type I pieces. Edit either lower boundary, drag a red strip, or sweep them, and watch the geometry and the limits of integration change together.
